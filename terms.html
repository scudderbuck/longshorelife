Chat rules

These rules apply to everything people post: chat messages, display names, reports, shared boards and shared gear cards and pictures.

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, bullying or personal attacks.
  2. No hate speech or slurs about anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or national origin.
  3. No threats or violence, and nothing that encourages violence or self-harm.
  4. No sexual or sexually explicit content, and nothing that sexualizes minors.
  5. No spam, scams, ads or repeated messages.
  6. Nothing illegal, and don't post other people's private information (addresses, phone numbers, ID or pay details) without permission.
  7. Don't pretend to be someone else, a union officer, the PMA or Longshore Life staff.

Zero tolerance and moderation

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Posts that break these rules are removed, and people who break them are muted in a room or banned from all chat rooms, without warning.

  • Report: tap Report on any message, or ⋯ More → Report this person. Room moderators and the app owner review reports, usually within 24 hours, and remove content or users that break the rules.
  • Block: tap Block on a message, or ⋯ More → Block this person. Their messages are hidden on your phone. Manage your list under Chat → Blocked people.
  • Contact moderation: email kingofthedocks.app@gmail.com. Include the room, the person's display name and roughly when they posted.
