Anton (regular, subset)
This folder has a small subset of Anton for the new-domain splash title
and tribute lockup. It includes A–Z, a–z, 0–9, comma, hyphen, space, and
the en dash, covering "LONGSHORE LIFE", "WORK THE WATERFRONT",
"IN HONOR OF", "HARRY BRIDGES", and "1901 – 1990". Anton is the closest
OFL match to the Waterfront shirt's heavy condensed sans block lettering
(compared with Bebas Neue, League Gothic, Oswald Bold, and Big Shoulders
Display Black).

The title fill is merch gold #ffda59 (sampled from the shirt letters)
with a distressed texture; the tagline is merch orange-red #ff3b00.
The tribute at the bottom of the new-host splash is a 3-line lockup:
"IN HONOR OF" and "1901 – 1990" in cream #f5ecd7, and "HARRY BRIDGES"
in safety orange #ff3b00 with the same grunge texture as the title.
