Anton (regular, subset)
This folder has a small subset of Anton for the new-domain splash title
and tribute line. It includes A–Z, a–z, 0–9, comma, hyphen, space, and
the en dash, covering "LONGSHORE LIFE", "WORK THE WATERFRONT", and
"In honor of Harry Bridges, 1901–1990". Anton is the closest OFL match
to the Waterfront shirt's heavy condensed sans block lettering (compared
with Bebas Neue, League Gothic, Oswald Bold, and Big Shoulders Display
Black).

Fill color is merch gold #ffda59 (sampled from the shirt letters) with a
distressed texture; the tagline is merch orange-red #ff3b00; the tribute
is cream #f5ecd7 at 70% opacity.
