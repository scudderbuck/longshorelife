Delete Your DataLongshore Life™

This page explains how to delete the data you created in Longshore Life™ (the web app at longshorelife.app and the Longshore Life Android app on Google Play). You don't need to install the app to ask for deletion.

No account is needed for most of the app. Hours, pay, notes and photos are stored only on your phone; we never receive them. Chat, shared boards and shared gear cards use an anonymous chat ID (no email or password) created the first time you open them.

Option 1: delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open Longshore Life and go to Chat.
  2. Tap Delete my chat data (under the chat rules, or in Change name / code / My ID).
  3. Read the list and tap Delete my chat data, then confirm.

Do this on each phone or browser you used for chat; each has its own chat ID.

Option 2: ask us by email

Email kingofthedocks.app@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data and include:

  • the display name you used in chat and the room (for example LOCAL19 or Coastwide),
  • roughly when you posted, and if you can, your My ID (Chat → Change name / code / My ID → Copy).

We'll confirm by email and delete the data within 30 days, usually much sooner. We may ask a question to make sure the request is really yours.

What is deleted

  • All chat messages you posted, and pins of them.
  • Reports you filed.
  • Your display name on shared ship gear cards and diagrams (the ship information itself stays for other workers, without your name).
  • Your anonymous chat ID (Firebase anonymous sign-in).
  • On your phone (in-app option): your chat name, room codes, accepted chat rules and block list.

Data on your phone (hours, pay, notes, photos, backups) is never on our servers. Delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

What may be kept

  • Mute and ban records (the chat ID, the display name at the time, who applied it and when) are kept to prevent abuse and ban evasion, until a moderator lifts them.
  • Reports about your messages filed by other people are kept until a moderator resolves them.
  • Shared boards you edited stay for the room; they don't show your name.
  • Visit counts (GoatCounter) are anonymous and can't be linked to you.
