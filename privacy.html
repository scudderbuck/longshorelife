Privacy PolicyLongshore Life™

Effective November 13, 2026. This policy covers the Longshore Life™ app at longshorelife.app and the Longshore Life Android app on Google Play, which opens the same web app. Longshore Life is a free beta tool for longshore workers. It is run by Longshore Life ("we") and is not made by, or affiliated with, any union, employer or the PMA.

Short version: your hours and pay stay on your phone. We don't sell data, show ads, or ask for an account. Optional online features only send what you type into them.

Data kept only on your device

  • Shifts, hours, jobs, pay estimates, paycheck comparisons, settings and notes you enter.
  • Photos you add (for example, ship letter or board photos) and automatic safety copies of your data.

This data is stored in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see it and it is not sent to us. Backups are files you choose to save or share yourself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data deletes it, so keep a backup.

Visit counts (GoatCounter)

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not store your IP address. It records things like the page viewed, the referring site, browser type, screen size and approximate country, so we can see how many people use the app.

Optional online features

Chat, shared boards, shared gear notes and diagrams, and merch reservations work only if you open and use them. They use Google Firebase with an anonymous sign-in (no email or password). What is stored on Firebase servers is only what you enter: the display name you choose, your messages, the boards, notes or diagrams you share, and reservation details (item, size, quantity, note and name). Other people in the same room can see it. You can delete your own messages and stop sharing boards in the app. Room moderators can remove messages that are reported.

Public read-only content

The app shows public information: ship letters posted to the hall's GroupMe group, news, and vessel lists. This content is read-only. The app does not connect to your GroupMe account and does not post anything for you.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. We don't run a push server or collect a device ID for notifications.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade any data, and we don't sell accounts.
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no location tracking, no access to your contacts.
  • No account is required to use the app.

Links to other sites

The app links to outside sites such as the Longshore Life shop (Fourthwall), the PMA, union and credit union sites. Shop product images load from Fourthwall. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is meant for adult workers and is not directed to children under 13.
